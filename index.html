03About

Most of my work is the part nobody notices when it goes well: one pipeline for two hundred projects at Clover, a platform quant developers build on at RBC, a cluster of a hundred nodes that ran on cloud servers, Jetsons and Raspberry Pis.

Before Ireland I co-founded Cvas in Tehran, grew its engineering team to thirty and took it through an acquisition, and helped start IrPycon and TehranLUG. EasyEnv came out of thousands of technical interviews: I wanted one that looks like the job does now, a real machine with AI in the room.
