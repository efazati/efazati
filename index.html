Mohammad Efazati

Principal DevOps Engineer · Tullamore, Ireland

I build infrastructure that keeps working when nobody is watching, and the teams that run it. I have been doing it since 2007, across more than fifty companies: payments at Clover, an internal developer platform for quant developers at Royal Bank of Canada, and a Kubernetes cluster of a hundred-odd nodes that ran from the cloud to ARM boards at the edge at Cainthus.

I am currently co-founding EasyEnv at Donedeploy. After running thousands of technical interviews I wanted one that looks like the job: a real Linux machine, AI allowed, and the whole session to review afterwards.

Before Ireland I co-founded Cvas in Tehran and led its engineering team of thirty through an acquisition, and started IrPycon and TehranLUG, two communities I still care about. I write about infrastructure and hiring.
