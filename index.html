About

My job is finding the solution. The problem can be anything.

My career started in 2007, but it really began the day I first saw Linux. I have tried to learn something new every day since.

I have been a full-stack developer, a DevOps engineer, a platform engineer, a co-founder and an engineering director with 30+ engineers. I have run edge clusters on ARM boards, built CI/CD for a payments company, and an internal platform for quant developers at a global bank. Different teams, different problems, the same instinct: understand the system, then make it simpler.

Along the way I ran thousands of technical interviews, enough to see exactly where hiring breaks. That is why I co-founded EasyEnv, and why I write about hiring as much as about infrastructure.

The fastest way to find a solution is to start building toward it. The answer sharpens with every iteration. // twenty years of method
  • 01Boring is a feature. The best infrastructure is the one nobody talks about.
  • 02Everything as code. If it was configured by hand, it will drift.
  • 03Paved roads. Teams should ship without learning the plumbing.
  • 04Loud failure. Silent breakage is the only kind that really hurts.
